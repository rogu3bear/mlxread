# Customer

## Job Statement

When reading on my Mac becomes tiring or interrupts other work, I want to hear
the selected passage in a clear, comfortable voice, so I can follow the meaning
without losing my place. This is inferred from the user's goal and README,
not a customer interview finding.

## Job Dimensions

| Dimension | Desired progress | Current underdelivery | Owner / priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Functional | Find a voice, set a comfortable pace, listen and stop | Voice codes hide language; native Kokoro timing is unused | TASK / P1 |
| Emotional | Trust each action and feel in control | Preview may download a missing model; tiny help text obscures recovery | TASK / P1 |
| Social | Read privately without needing assistance | Permission and privacy explanations require technical interpretation | TASK / P2 |

Daily use (Little Hire) is the priority. First use (Big Hire) still needs a
clear download → preview → select-and-listen path. No funnel data establishes
which step loses the most people.

## Competing Alternatives

| Alternative | Why hired | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
| macOS Speak Selection | Already available from a shortcut | May not match the listener's preferred voice |
| Copy into another reader | Explicit playback and voice choice | Context switching and clipboard work |
| Read silently | No setup | Does not relieve reading fatigue |
| Skip the passage (non-consumption) | Avoid effort | Loses the information |

## Evidence after this pass

The native walkthrough confirms voice names, language-matched samples, reachable
preview/stop, readable form text and immediate setup reopening. Kokoro's native
timing changes audio duration without a second playback-speed multiplier. These
observations address the source underdelivery above; they do not establish a
listener's preferred voice or a retention effect.

| Remaining question | Owner | Priority | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which available voice and pace is most comfortable for the listener? | Product owner | P2 | Deferred to a listening comparison; no automatic collection |
