# Positioning & Messaging

## Competitive Alternatives

macOS Speak Selection, another reader, silent reading, and skipping the text;
see CUSTOMER.md. No competitive superiority is established by this pass.

## Unique Attributes → Value Themes

| Attribute | Value ("so what") | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Local synthesis | Listen without sending reading content elsewhere | docs/privacy.md and NativeMLXSpeechEngine |
| Selection shortcut | Stay with the original passage | Existing hotkey/capture architecture |
| Voice and pace choices | Find a comfortable listening setting | Local preview and routing checks; preference unmeasured |

## Best-Fit Customer

A Mac user who wants selected text read aloud privately with minimal interruption.

## Market Category

Existing category: native text-to-speech utility. No new category claim is needed.

## One-Liner

Hear selected text in a voice and pace that suit you, right on your Mac.

## Brand Script (StoryBrand)

Character: a reader. Problem: reading fatigue and interrupted concentration.
Guide: a small native utility. Plan: choose a voice, preview, select text and
press Option–Escape. Call to action: Preview Voice. Failure: confusing setup or
uncomfortable speech. Success: follow the passage at a comfortable pace.

## Key Messages

SUCCESs shorthand: Simple/Unexpected/Concrete/Credible/Emotional/Stories, each
0–2. These are editorial assessments, not user-test scores; surprise and story
are intentionally low in utility controls.
